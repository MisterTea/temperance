## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: primary symbol for Temperance, an app and website that helps people regulate internet and social media use.
Design a memorable, highly reduced logo symbol inspired by the cardinal virtue of temperance: chosen limits, balance, measured use. Create a bold, elegant geometric T whose crossbar is a shallow open bowl with gently upturned ends, joined seamlessly to a sturdy central stem. The bowl alludes subtly to the vessel traditionally associated with temperance, while the whole silhouette reads immediately as T. One unified solid silhouette, optically balanced and exceptionally simple. The bowl is broad and shallow; the stem short and confident, with softly rounded lower corners; substantial strokes and generous open negative space. Aim for a distinctive contemporary app identity with warmth and authority. It must remain recognizable at 16–32 pixels and in a single ink. No tiny details, hairlines, gradients, outlines, shadows, ornaments, shields, locks, phones, clocks, leaves, scales or religious imagery. Deep evergreen solid ink. Center the single symbol, large with comfortable margins, on a genuinely transparent background. No wordmark, letters other than the implied T, captions, border, mockup, grid or presentation board. Crisp flat vector-like edges.
