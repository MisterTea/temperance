# Temperance logo — revised direction

A geometric T with a split stem suggesting the pause symbol: deliberate interruption and self-control. The straight crossbar removes the horn-like silhouette of the first concept. Midnight indigo replaces evergreen.

Asset: `temperance-mark-v2.png` (transparent PNG). Created with the built-in image generation tool. Existing app and site assets were not changed.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: logo symbol for Temperance, an internet and social media usage regulation app and website.
Primary request: Create a refined, distinctive, extremely simple logo that combines a capital T with the pause symbol, suggesting a deliberate pause and self-control. The previous concept had an upward-curving bowl and looked like bull horns; this revision must have an entirely different silhouette.
Subject: A single compact geometric T monogram. One thick perfectly straight horizontal top bar with subtly rounded corners. Under the crossbar are two short parallel thick vertical stems separated by a generous straight vertical negative-space slot, so the lower part reads as a pause symbol and the whole reads as T. Center both stems under the bar. The two stems together occupy approximately half of the full width. Balanced square overall proportions, confident weight, gently softened corners, optically adjusted. Exactly three simple solid rectangles joined into one silhouette through the top bar. The open-bottom slot must stay clear at 16–32px. No enclosing shape.
Style: Professional modern brand identity, clean flat single-color vector-like mark, crisp edges, no gradients, textures, shadows, outlines, 3D or hairlines.
Color: Solid deep midnight indigo, approximately #303A70.
Composition: One large centered symbol on genuinely transparent background, with comfortable equal margins.
Constraints: No curves resembling horns, no bowl, no cup, no animal, no upward-flaring ends, no shield, no phone, no clock, no leaf, no play triangle. No text, captions, mockups, borders, brand sheet or extra marks. This is the standalone symbol only. Recognizable at postage-stamp size.
