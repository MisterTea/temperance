WHY WE CREATED TEMPERANCE

Parenting is enough.
Managing screens
should feel simpler.

We created Temperance to help parents manage screen-time for their kids. Screens are part of everyday life, but so are homework, sleep, play, and time together.

Parents need a practical way to set boundaries that reflect their family’s routines: a daily budget, a predictable bedtime, or a pause for dinner.

We also believe families should control the tools they rely on. That’s why Temperance is open source and runs on your own server, with no Temperance cloud account and no telemetry.

HOW IT WORKS

Your family’s rules.
One home base.

Temperance brings a parent dashboard and a VPN gateway together in one program you run.

  1. 1

    Set up your home base

    Run Temperance on Linux, macOS, or Windows, or use Docker. Follow the setup guide to make the gateway reachable.

  2. 2

    Connect the family

    Create profiles and enroll devices. VPN apps connect supported phones and computers to home, even when they’re away.

  3. 3

    Find your balance

    Choose limits, schedules, and filters from the dashboard. Use home-network DNS or Linux router mode for TVs and consoles.

Built for families comfortable with a little setup.

Temperance is a working MVP. Remote VPN access needs a reachable server and a forwarded UDP port. App source is available for macOS, Windows, Android, and iOS; signed app-store releases are not yet available. Time budgets measure observed network minutes, rather than all time a screen is on. VPN removal, encrypted DNS, and other bypasses remain limitations.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things to know.

Is Temperance free?

Temperance’s code is free and open source under the MIT license. You provide the computer or server, internet connection, and any hosting services you choose to use.

Does it work outside our home?

Enrolled devices using the Temperance VPN connect back to your gateway, so its rules can apply away from home. The tunnel must be active and your server reachable. Home-network DNS and router controls only apply on the network they serve.

Does this replace conversations about screen-time?

Temperance helps you put family agreements into practice. It works best alongside clear expectations and ongoing conversations about when, why, and how your kids use the internet.
